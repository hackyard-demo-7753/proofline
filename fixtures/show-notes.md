# Show notes (draft) - Six Minute Stories, ep. 12

This week we visit Crumb & Co., where Ravi and his team of twelve
bakers have baked every morning for fifty years.

Ravi's mother opened the shop in 1986, and the oven is older than he
is. On Saturdays they sell about three hundred loaves.

On the anniversary morning, the shop gives away free buns, just like
his mother did on opening day.
